# Form design

A minimal work surface in warm paper and charcoal, with an expressive serif heading and precise sans-serif controls. One plain-text canvas becomes one editable BlockNote document. The source remains available for comparison, and one Markdown copy action carries the result out. The document holds the visual emphasis.

The layout works on desktop and mobile. Motion respects reduced-motion preferences and does not delay access to the result. Reset clears the draft and returns to the paste surface.
